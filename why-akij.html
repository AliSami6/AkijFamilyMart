Good To Know

Questions? We've Got Answers

How can I be sure the products are 100% authentic?
Every item is sourced directly from the official brand or its authorized distributor. You can verify the batch code, expiry and hologram on arrival — and if anything looks off, we collect it back free of charge.
Which areas get the 60-minute express delivery?
Express slots are live in Gulshan, Banani, Dhanmondi, Uttara and Mirpur. Outside these zones we deliver within 24–48 hours nationwide.
What is your return & refund policy?
You have 7 days to return any unused, unopened product — no questions asked. Refunds are processed to your original payment method within 2–3 business days.
Why are your prices lower than physical supershops?
We operate direct-to-consumer, so there is no outlet rent and no middleman margin stacked on top. That saving is passed straight to you.
